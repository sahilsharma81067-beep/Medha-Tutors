ABOUT

Tutoring for all, regardless of ability

I have been tutoring maths for around 2 years, most notably with MyTutor.co.uk, helping several students achieve their goals and improve their confidence in the subject.

As someone not from a pure maths background, I have experience in applications of maths, and understand some students frustration in feeling that what they are learning is not applicable.

This is why I started Medha Tutors

I hope that with my experience, I can demonstrate the usefulness of mathematics in real life, and help students develop a genuine understanding of the subject.

Here at Medha Tutors, we provide tutoring for students across all exam boards and levels, from KS2 all the way to A-Level.

Our focus is on helping students develop genuine understanding rather than simply learning procedures. Lessons are tailored to the student's level, with particular attention to difficult problems, fundamentals and exam technique.

- Sahil Sharma, Founder
