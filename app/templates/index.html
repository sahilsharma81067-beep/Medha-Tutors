ABOUT

Maths tutoring for all, regardless of ability.

I have been tutoring Mathematics for around 2 years, most notably with MyTutor.co.uk, helping several students achieve their goals and improve their confidence in the subject.

As someone not from a pure maths background, I have experience in applied mathematics, and understand some students frustration in feeling that what they are learning is not applicable.

This is why I started Tutor4U

I hope that with my experience, I can demonstrate the usefulness of mathematics in real life, and help students develop a genuine understanding of the subject.

I offer tutoring for students across all exam boards and levels, from KS2 all the way to A-Level.

I focus on helping students develop genuine understanding rather than simply learning procedures. Tutor4U lessons are tailored to the student's level, with particular attention to difficult problems, fundamentals and exam technique.
